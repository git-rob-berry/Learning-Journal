### What business rules did we encode as CHECK, UNIQUE, and FOREIGN KEY, and why keep them in the database layer?
1. CHECK was used to make sure data follows certain rules. For example, a flight must arrive after it departs, and a booking status must be PENDING, CONFIRMED or CANCELLED.
2. UNIQUE was used to prevent duplicates. For example, two airports cannot have the same IATA code and two customers cannot have the same email address.
3. FOREIGN KEY / REFERENCES was used to make sure relationships between tables are valid. For example, a booking can only reference a customer and flight that actually exist.
4. Keeping these rules in the database itself helps maintain data quality regardless of what application or process is entering the data.
